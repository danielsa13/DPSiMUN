Greetings Delegates,

Welcome to UNODC at DPSIMUN ’26! My name is Michael, and I will be serving as your Chair. This is my fourth MUN overall, but my very first time sitting on this side of the desk. After three conferences as a delegate where I managed to win two awards, I have finally traded the stress of drafting long working papers for a gavel. The UNODC sits right at the front lines of tackling organized crime, cyberthreats, and illicit networks. Our job in this committee is to bring sharp, practical solutions to issues that international law often struggles to keep up with.

My main expectation is that you show up ready to actually debate rather than just read off a screen. Focus on substance, challenge weak arguments respectfully, and do not be afraid to take risks. If you trip up on a speech or mess up procedure, laugh it off and keep moving. Do your research, know your country policy inside out, and let us make this committee one to remember.

See you in session!
