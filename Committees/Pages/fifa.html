Founded in 1904, FIFA is the global governing body for association football, representing 211 national associations. It oversees the Laws of the Game, organizes international competitions such as the FIFA World Cups, and supports football development worldwide.

In line with the conference theme, "Engineering Stability in an Age of Volatility," FIFA serves as a platform for exploring how strong governance, innovation, and international cooperation can safeguard the integrity, resilience, and global unity of football amid evolving political, economic, and technological challenges.
