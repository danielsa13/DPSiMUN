SPECPOL

Committee Overview

DPSIMUN 2026 presents the Special Political and Decolonization Committee, where borders and sovereignty collide! With political disputes and peacekeeping on the line, delegates will untangle the conflicts that have divided nations for decades.

Formed in 1990, SPECPOL addresses political and decolonization-related topics including Palestinian refugees, space exploration, atomic radiation effects, peacekeeping, and humanitarian crises in non-self-governing territories.

In alignment with "Engineering Stability in an Age of Volatility," SPECPOL tasks delegates with engineering sustainable structural mechanisms to safeguard peace, mitigate regional instability, and protect human dignity.

Topics:
  1. Addressing the rise of State-sponsored proxy warfare over strategic resources and regional influence
    • Competition for Resources and Regional Influence
    • Weaponisation of Humanitarian Aid and Intelligence
    • Loopholes in Foreign Military Assistance
    • Funding militant groups and non-state actors
  2. Sanction evasion and Smuggling resources from the black market
