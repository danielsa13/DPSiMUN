CSTD

Committee Overview

DPSIMUN 2026 presents the Commission on Science and Technology for Development, where innovation meets responsibility! With progress and access on the line, delegates will decide how technology can lift nations without leaving anyone behind.

Established in 1992 under ECOSOC, CSTD acts as the primary UN focal point for science, technology, and innovation. It provides guidance on how innovation can drive the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, focusing on SDG 9 and SDG 17.

Under the theme "Radical Resilience: Engineering Stability in an Age of Volatility," CSTD serves as an indispensable forum for foresight, helping nations build technical policies capable of handling global disruptions.

Topics:
  1. Governance of Frontier Technologies for Crisis Response
    • Protection Against the Use of Dual-Use Tech
    • Establishing a Global Rapid Response Governance Council
    • Mitigating Dual-Use Dilemmas & The Global Tech-Divide
  2. Decentralised Power for a Volatile Future
    • The Geopolitics of Energy Decentralisation
    • Bridging the Energy Digital Divide
    • Managing the Vulnerable Edge in Security of Things
