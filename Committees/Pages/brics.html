BRICS Plus+

Committee Overview

DPSIMUN 2026 introduces BRICS Plus, the bloc rewriting the rules of global power! With trade, currency, and influence on the line, delegates will push for a bigger seat at the table and a louder say in how the world runs.

BRICS+ represents an expanded bloc of major emerging market economies aiming to promote multipolarity, economic cooperation, and systemic reform of global financial structures.

In line with "Engineering Stability in an Age of Volatility," BRICS+ serves as a critical forum for member states to design mechanisms that enhance economic resilience and reduce external dependencies.

Topic: Increasing local currency usage and reducing reliance on the US Dollar
  • Creation of a BRICS Common Currency or a BRICS Currency Unit (basket currency) — feasibility and challenges
  • Reducing vulnerability to US sanctions through de-dollarization
  • Balancing de-dollarization with maintaining economic stability and investor confidence
