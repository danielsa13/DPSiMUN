UNODC

Committee Overview

DPSIMUN 2026 presents the United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime, where the law chases what hides in the shadows! With trafficking, organized crime, and corruption on the line, delegates will track the threats that cross every border.

The rapid evolution of decentralized tech, advanced drug trafficking channels, and machine learning has given transnational syndicates a massive upgrade, turning global crime into a highly volatile, borderless enterprise. Operating out of Vienna since 1997, UNODC acts as the central hub for global legal cooperation, trend analysis, and tactical training.

Tying directly into "Radical Resilience: Engineering Stability in an Age of Volatility," UNODC forces delegates to confront a stark reality: traditional law enforcement is losing the tech and tactical race. Delegates must pioneer adaptive, cross-border regulatory frameworks and defensive protocols to out-engineer modern crime.

Topics:
  1. Engineering Global Legal Shields Against Forced Labor and Human Trafficking in Crisis Zones
    • Strengthening international labor regulations and supply chain transparency
    • Protecting migrant workers and refugees at high-risk border crossings
    • Clamping down on online job scams and cyber-trafficking rings
    • Preventing the corruption of border control and law enforcement officials
  2. Combating Organized Crime Governance and Territorial Control in Volatile Conflict Zones
    • Dismantling armed cartels and shadow legal systems in unstable regions
    • Targeting financial revenue streams and extortion networks of local gangs
    • Improving maritime security and naval patrolling to stop illegal arms shipments
    • Providing economic support programs to reduce community reliance on criminal groups
