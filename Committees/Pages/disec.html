DISEC

Committee Overview

DPSIMUN 2026 introduces the Disarmament and International Security Committee, where global peace hangs by a thread! With weapons and world security on the line, delegates will debate under pressure and hold the balance that keeps nations safe.

DISEC is the First Committee of the United Nations General Assembly, tasked with addressing global challenges related to international peace, security, and the regulation of armaments. DISEC serves as a central platform for multilateral dialogue on disarmament, conflict prevention, and emerging threats.

In line with "Engineering Stability in an Age of Volatility," DISEC provides a forum to examine how effective diplomacy, strategic cooperation, and innovative policy frameworks can strengthen global security amid rapid technological advancements.

Topics:
  1. Regulating Autonomous Weapons Systems and Drone Warfare
    • Accountability and legal responsibility in the use of autonomous weapons
    • Ethical implications of removing human control from warfare
    • Preventing proliferation of drone technology to non-state actors
    • Establishing international frameworks for lethal autonomous weapons
  2. Strengthening Arms Control in an Era of Technological and Geopolitical Instability
    • The erosion of existing arms control and non-proliferation agreements
    • Integrating emerging technologies (AI, cyber tools) into frameworks
    • Confidence-building measures between major powers
    • Enhancing verification and enforcement mechanisms
