SECURITY COUNCIL

Committee Overview

DPSIMUN 2026 introduces the Security Council, the most powerful and high-stakes committee of the UN! With global peace and security on the line, delegates will face crises, negotiate under pressure, and make decisions that could change the course of history.

The UNSC bears primary responsibility for maintaining international peace and security. Comprising 5 permanent veto-bearing members and 10 elected non-permanent members, its resolutions carry binding legal authority.

Guided by "Radical Resilience: Engineering Stability in the Age of Volatility," delegates must adapt Council mechanisms to manage evolving armed conflicts, geopolitical rivalries, and technological shifts.

Topics:
  1. Addressing Regional Escalation and the Threat to International Peace in the Middle East
    • Preventing further escalation between Israel and Iran
    • Protecting civilians and critical infrastructure during regional conflict
    • Maritime security in the Persian Gulf and Strait of Hormuz
    • Diplomacy in preventing wider regional war
  2. Responding to the Humanitarian and Security Crisis in Sudan
    • Protection of civilians and humanitarian access
    • Preventing regional spillover and instability
    • Ceasefire negotiations and peacekeeping efforts
    • Accountability for violations of international humanitarian law

Dear Delegates,

Welcome to DPS International Model United Nations 2026. It is an honor to serve as your Chair, and I am delighted to welcome you to this committee. Every MUN conference presents an opportunity to step into the shoes of world leaders, tackle complex international challenges, and discover the power of diplomacy. I hope this committee will encourage you to think beyond the obvious, question assumptions, and engage in meaningful debate. Our theme, "Radical Resilience: Engineering Stability in an Age of Volatility", calls on us to recognize that uncertainty is a defining feature of today's world. As delegates, you will be challenged to balance national interests with global responsibility, negotiate with purpose, and seek solutions that are both practical and impactful.

I look forward to meeting each of you and witnessing the insightful discussions and innovative ideas you will bring to committee. I wish you the very best in your preparation, and I hope your experience at DPS I MUN 2026 is both enjoyable and inspiring!
