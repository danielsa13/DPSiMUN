ABOUT THE CONFERENCE

What is DPS MUN?

Why DPSI MUN?

DPS Model United Nations (DPSIMUN) is one of Ghana’s biggest high school Model United Nations conferences, bringing together young leaders for three days of intense debate, meaningful networking, and an unforgettable experience.

Under this year’s theme, “Radical Resilience: Engineering Stability in an Age of Volatility,” delegates will tackle pressing global challenges while building connections and developing their diplomatic skills.

And when the debates come to an end, delegates can unwind and celebrate at our amazing social evening, creating memories that last beyond the conference.

This year, DPSIMUN is also introducing an environmental initiative for the first time, partnering with Green Advocacy Ghana to tackle food waste generated by such large-scale events through composting.

So, what are you waiting for? Come join us at DPSIMUN’2026 for three unforgettable days of debate, diplomacy and positive change!
